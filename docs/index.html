The Reels Rule

Charles has exactly one job, and she takes it extremely seriously.

  1. You open Reels, Shorts or TikTok and start scrolling. Charles notices. Charles always notices.
  2. At 4:00 she says "one more minute of reels..." This is your warning.
  3. At 5:00 she sprints over, leaps up, hangs off your browser tab and swats it closed.
  4. She lands and says something like "go touch grass :3". You're welcome.

She never guesses

Before she swats, she checks the address bar really says reels (the exact site and page, not just the word), that the tab she's aiming at is the selected one and matches that page, and then finds the tab again right before pressing its close button, in case you switched tabs. If she can't be sure, she closes nothing: she tells you she couldn't find the reels tab safely and blocks scrolling instead.

Adopt Charles

Windows 10 or 11. One file, no Python, no installer. Takes about ten seconds.

1

Get her

Download Charles.exe and double-click it. She appears in the bottom right of your screen, says hi, and shows you a quick tour, where you can put her on your desktop, start her with Windows and let her check for updates.

Windows releases are code-signed for free through the SignPath Foundation (see the code signing policy). Until the first signed release is out, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway. Rather build her yourself? It's all on GitHub.

2

Make it your rule

Right-click her → Reels rules to pick the time limit, the warning and what she does. Everything from the tour is in there too.

3

Test the Reels Rule

Right-click her → Test the reels mission. Then open any reel: she closes it after 20 seconds.

Prefer running her from source?

What she watches

Charles keeps an eye on your browsing, so here's exactly what she sees and what she does with it.

👀 What she looks at

  • The title and program name of the window in front (and the one under your mouse), once a second.
  • That browser's address bar text, to see if it's a reels page.
  • That you scrolled, not what you scrolled past.
  • The browser's tab strip, only at the moment she closes a tab.
  • Your cursor, so her eyes can follow it.

📦 What leaves your computer

Nothing.

No analytics, no telemetry. The only connection she ever makes is a once-a-day update check with GitHub, and only if you turn on Check for updates. Off, she never goes online. She keeps a reels timer in memory and never stores URLs, titles or history. The only file she writes is her settings (skin, coat, size, reels rules).

🔑 What she needs

  • No admin rights. She runs as you.
  • Windows UI Automation, the accessibility API screen readers use, to read the address bar and press close.
  • Mouse & keyboard hooks to notice scrolling. She ignores every key except the ones that move a reels feed (arrows, Page, Home, End, Space, J, K), only while reels is in front, and never records what you type.
  • One startup entry in the registry, only if you tick Start with Windows.
  • A desktop icon, only if you ask for one.
  • One trip to GitHub a day to look for a newer her, only if you turn on Check for updates.

Privacy policy: Charles collects no personal data. This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

Frequently asked questions

Why is she called Charles?

She chose it. We don't ask questions.

What happened to Charles the Ist?

Knocked a full glass of water onto a keyboard, looked directly at the camera, and quietly abdicated.

Can Charles be a dog?

Yes! Right-click → Skin → Puppy. He woofs when you poke him, wags when you pet him, and still closes your reels. Switch back to Kitty any time.

Is she made of chocolate?

No. Please do not eat Charles.

Does she spy on me?

She reads your browser's address bar to see if it's a reels page, and that's it. Nothing ever leaves your computer. She's a cat, not a data broker. Here's everything she looks at.

Which sites count as reels?

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc, Zen, Chromium, Thorium, LibreWolf, Waterfox or Floorp, plus the TikTok and Instagram desktop apps.

I just had the reel open, I wasn't scrolling. Does that count?

Nope. Only active scrolling counts, and taking a break of over a minute resets the timer. She's strict, not unreasonable.

What if she can't tell which tab is reels?

Then she doesn't close anything. She tells you she couldn't find it safely and blocks scrolling instead. Closing the wrong tab would be far worse than closing none.

How do I update her?

Turn on Check for updates (in her tour, or right-click her) and she'll tell you when there's a new version, then fetch it and swap herself over when you say so. Or download the new Charles.exe and open it: the old Charles steps aside. Either way your coat, size and rules stay put.

Five minutes is too strict / not strict enough.

Right-click → Reels rules. Pick 2 to 30 minutes, how much warning you get, and whether she closes the tab, blocks scrolling or just tells you off.

Can I make her stop?

Right-click → Bye Charles. She'll forgive you. Eventually.
