The Reels Rule

Charles has exactly one job, and she takes it extremely seriously.

  1. You open Reels, Shorts or TikTok and start scrolling. Charles notices. Charles always notices.
  2. At 4:00 she says "one more minute of reels..." This is your warning.
  3. At 5:00 she sprints over, leaps up, hangs off your browser tab and swats it closed.
  4. She lands and says something like "go touch grass :3". You're welcome.

Adopt Charles

Windows 10 or 11, plus Python 3.10 or newer. Takes about a minute.

1

Get her

Download the zip and unzip it, or clone the repo:

git clone https://github.com/yad2thisyazm/charles-chocolate-the-charles-the-2nd.git
cd charles-chocolate-the-charles-the-2nd
2

Give her snacks

She runs on Pillow (for drawing) and uiautomation (for reading your address bar):

pip install -r requirements.txt
3

Let her out

pythonw desk_cat.py

She appears in the bottom right of your screen and says hi. To have her start with Windows, put a shortcut in shell:startup.

4

Test the Reels Rule

Right-click her → Test the reels mission. Then open any reel: she closes it after 20 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Why is she called Charles?

She chose it. We don't ask questions.

What happened to Charles the Ist?

Knocked a full glass of water onto a keyboard, looked directly at the camera, and quietly abdicated.

Is she made of chocolate?

No. Please do not eat Charles.

Does she spy on me?

She reads your browser's address bar to see if it's a reels page, and that's it. Nothing ever leaves your computer. She's a cat, not a data broker.

Which sites count as reels?

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc or Zen, plus the TikTok and Instagram desktop apps.

I just had the reel open, I wasn't scrolling. Does that count?

Nope. Only active scrolling counts, and taking a break of over a minute resets the timer. She's strict, not unreasonable.

Can I make her stop?

Right-click → Bye Charles. She'll forgive you. Eventually.
